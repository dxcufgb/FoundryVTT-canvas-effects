# Changelog

## 0.3.1

- Optional support for Dxcufgb's Debug Recorder: when it is installed and recording, canvas effects adds a snapshot of its state (areas and layers by effect, how much is drawn, lights, sound loops, whether effects are on) and short breadcrumbs of its main actions to the recording. Nothing changes without the recorder, and no names, text, image or sound paths, or addresses are recorded.
