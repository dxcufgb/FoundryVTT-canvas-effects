# Sound credits

Every sound in this folder is CC0 or public domain: you may use, change and share it, also
commercially, without credit. The credits below are a courtesy and a record of where each file
comes from.

The ambience loops were made mono and brought to about the same loudness (−20 LUFS, light peak
limiting); the long recordings were cut to about a minute, with the cut crossfaded so the loop has
no seam. Nothing else was changed.

## Where the files were taken from

- The rubberduck packs were taken from the copy in
  [lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds](https://github.com/lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds)
  (commit `f2b6264`), which keeps the packs whole, under their OpenGameArt names; the pack contents
  match the descriptions on OpenGameArt. All of rubberduck's uploads there are CC0.
- The Blanket recordings were taken from
  [rafaelmardojai/blanket](https://github.com/rafaelmardojai/blanket) (commit `775f2a7`); their
  sources and licences are those in Blanket's `SOUNDS_LICENSING.md`. Blanket's other sounds (rain,
  storm, waves, city, train) are CC BY and are **not** used here.
- **Check before a release:** the five Blanket recordings' licences come from Blanket's list; they
  could not be checked on Freesound and SoundBible themselves when they were added (those sites were
  out of reach). Open the five source pages above and confirm the licence shown there.
