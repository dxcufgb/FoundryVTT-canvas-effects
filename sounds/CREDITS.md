# Sound credits

Every sound in this folder is CC0 or public domain: you may use, change and share it, also
commercially, without credit. The credits below are a courtesy and a record of where each file
comes from.

The ambience loops were made mono and brought to about the same loudness (−20 LUFS, light peak
limiting); the long recordings were cut to about a minute, with the cut crossfaded so the loop has
no seam. The bat, rat and spider loops were put together from short clips, scattered over 24 s so
they loop without a seam. The thunder was cut to start just before each crack or roll, 10–14 s long
with a fade-out, made mono and peak-normalised. Nothing else was changed.

## Where the files were taken from

- The rubberduck packs were taken from the copy in
  [lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds](https://github.com/lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds)
  (commit `f2b6264`), which keeps the packs whole, under their OpenGameArt names; the pack contents
  match the descriptions on OpenGameArt. All of rubberduck's uploads there are CC0.
- The Blanket recordings were taken from
  [rafaelmardojai/blanket](https://github.com/rafaelmardojai/blanket) (commit `775f2a7`); their
  sources and licences are those in Blanket's `SOUNDS_LICENSING.md`. Blanket's other sounds (rain,
  storm, waves, city, train) are CC BY and are **not** used here.
- The five Blanket recordings' licences were checked on their source pages on 2026-10-18: the
  three Freesound sounds are marked CC0 there, and the two SoundBible sounds Public Domain, with the
  authors named above.
- The kmontesdev and the newer OpenGameArt sounds were downloaded on 2026-10-18 from their official
  pages above (the kmontesdev pack from the Google Drive folder its itch.io page links to). Each page
  states CC0: the kmontesdev pack as a whole ("CC0 Public Domain"), each OpenGameArt entry in its
  License field, and Droning Sound Effects also says the files are public domain. None came from a
  local library and none was generated.
- The nine Freesound thunder recordings were downloaded on 2026-10-18 from their pages above, signed
  in, as the original files; each page shows the CC0 licence.
- The sounds from `morning-birds.ogg` to `desert-night.ogg` in the table above were taken from the
  originals kept with the *The Thirteen Forgotten* adventure module (its `tools/generator/recordings/
  _sourced/`, where each is recorded in `SOURCES.md`). Each source page was checked again on
  2026-10-18 and shows CC0.
